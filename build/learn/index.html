How the Game Works

1) Sign up

Create your account, pick a topic and a level that suits you. It takes under a minute.

2) Enter with Credits

Use any credit type (Earned, Donated, Referral, Purchased) to enter a quiz—simple and flexible.

3) Answer & Win

Correct answers win credits back—often more than you spent—so practice really pays.

4) Improve Weekly

Climb levels across your Category → Subject → Level triplets and aim for the weekly improvers list.

All About Credits

  • Four kinds: Earned, Donated, Referral, Purchased.
  • Use any type to enter quizzes.
  • Winnings are added to Earned Credits only.

Earned Credits are the ones you redeem for prizes during scheduled checkout windows.

Winning Prizes

  • Digital gift cards or physical items.
  • Add to cart anytime; checkout during weekly windows.
  • Eligibility needs enough Earned Credits and a top improver rank.

Collect, improve, and redeem—steady progress is rewarded.

About the App

Runs Smooth on Low-End Android

Turn on the lightweight mode to play comfortably on entry-level phones. It dials down animations, limits effects, and trims background activity so the app stays responsive.

  • Suppress non-essential animations and heavy transitions.
  • Prefer static visuals and reduced motion where possible.
  • Optimized for low memory and CPU spikes.

Themes for Every Player

Switch looks without changing your flow. Choose what feels right for your eyes and mood.

  • Default — clean and bright for everyday use.
  • Dark — low-glare, night-friendly colors.
  • Kids — colorful, playful visuals for young learners.

WebAssembly on Chrome

The web version runs in WASM mode on Chrome for near-native speed. Firefox is not supported right now.

  • Best experience on the latest Chrome.
  • Enable hardware acceleration for smoother play.
  • If a page looks stuck, try clearing cached workers.

Data Protection

Sensitive user data is encrypted in storage and protected in transit. We follow least-privilege access for systems that need it.

  • Encrypted at rest for sensitive fields.
  • Transport security for all network calls.
  • Device-level safety checks where available.

Under-13 Parental Consent

Players under 13 will need verified parental consent. Verification is required to collect prizes, purchase credits, and complete certain account actions.

  • Parent/guardian approval before verification steps.
  • Verification checks apply when redeeming prizes.
  • Extra safeguards for billing-related features.
