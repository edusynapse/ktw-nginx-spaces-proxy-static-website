How sponsorship works

A four-step workflow from registration to live ads.

01

Register a sponsor profile

Provide your brand name, description, logo, image assets, and landing page URL. This becomes the base record for all your campaigns.

02

Submit ad message creatives

Each creative includes a title, body copy, image, and call-to-action. You can submit multiple messages targeting different audience segments.

03

Platform review

Platform administrators review each creative. Approved messages go live. Returned submissions receive a written reason and can be revised and resubmitted.

04

Ads go live

Approved messages appear linked to relevant prize items — reaching learners at the moment they are considering or redeeming a reward.

Reach the right audience

Scope your messages by age, language, country, and education level.

Know To Win's demographic targeting lets sponsors narrow their reach to the learner segments most relevant to them. A school-supplies brand can target primary and secondary school learners; a professional-certification body can target university and postgraduate cohorts.

Age range

Set a minimum and maximum age to ensure your message reaches the right life stage — from primary school learners to working professionals.

Languages

Target learners using one or more supported languages — letting you align your message language with your audience's preferred language.

Countries

Restrict your ad to one or more countries to stay relevant to your operating geography and avoid wasted impressions.

Education levels

Scope by education level — primary, secondary, tertiary, postgraduate — so your message lands with the cohort it is designed for.

Track performance

View counts, click counts, and per-day engagement — per message.

Global totals

View and click totals across all your ad messages give you an at-a-glance measure of overall campaign performance.

Per-day breakdown

A daily engagement hash tracks views and clicks day by day, so you can see when your messages land and how performance trends over time.

Per-message stats

Individual view and click counts per creative let you compare performance between messages and refine your targeting or copy accordingly.

Content workflow

Every ad creative goes through a submission and approval workflow.

Sponsored content is never shown to learners before a platform administrator has reviewed and approved it. This keeps the experience safe and consistent for all learners on the platform.

Submit for review

Once you submit a message creative, it enters a review queue. Your submission status is visible in your sponsor dashboard.

Revise and resubmit

If a submission is returned, the reason is included. Revise the creative and resubmit — the review cycle continues until approved.

Enable or disable at any time

After approval, you can enable or disable individual messages without going through the review cycle again.
