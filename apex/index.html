FAQ

Is this the app itself?

No. This is the public website on the apex domain. The live web app is hosted separately at app.knowtowin.com.

Why split the site and the app?

The public homepage can stay lightweight, indexable, and easy to share, while the application can keep its own routing, caching, and runtime behavior.

Can this page grow later?

Yes. This is a starting point. More sections, screenshots, school-specific messaging, legal pages, and analytics can be added without touching the app bundle.
