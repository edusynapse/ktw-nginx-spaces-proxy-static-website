How your brand shows up

Alongside rewards. Within the learning experience.

Prize sponsorship and advertising work together to give your brand a presence on the platform.

Prize recognition

Be associated with the prizes you support

Your approved sponsor name and logo can appear in sponsor listings for products where you still have unused sponsored units. Your contribution becomes part of the prize’s story.

Reviewed advertising

Tell learners what your brand offers

Approved messages can appear in app ad placements, including prize details. Messages are matched to an approved age range and language, with a platform-hosted landing experience for younger audiences.

Placement depends on audience eligibility, available sponsored stock, and credit balance. Ad-free members do not see ads, and kids mode hides the prize sponsor strip. Sponsorship does not guarantee a number of views, clicks, or sales.

From interest to participation

A clear path to becoming a sponsor.

Start with your brand and the prizes you would like to support. Then prepare the message you want eligible learners to see.

  1. 01

    Create your sponsor profile

    Introduce your brand and provide your details and assets for approval. Prize purchases are available to approved adult sponsors.

  2. 02

    Choose from the platform’s prize catalog

    Sponsor products already approved and listed on Know To Win, using purchased credits. New products are onboarded by the platform before they can be sponsored. Units become available for prize allocation after an administrator marks the sponsor order as received.

  3. 03

    Submit your message for review

    Prepare your creative and any required landing content. The platform reviews your message and approves its age range before it can be served.

  4. 04

    Follow performance and keep participating

    Review views, clicks, and credit usage in sponsor reporting. Replenish your prize inventory and credit balance to remain eligible for advertising.

Two things to budget for

Prize inventory: the sponsored product units you purchase for the prize pool.

Advertising: views and authorized clicks can incur separate purchased-credit charges, subject to current rates and sponsor discounts.

New ad selection requires unused sponsored stock and the minimum purchased-credit balance. When your stock is fully allocated or your balance falls below the minimum, new ad selection pauses.

Check current prices, discounts, and balance requirements in the app before committing your budget.

The practical details

A few things worth knowing.

Does my business have to sell learning products?

No. Learning-related products may have immediate relevance, while other brands may want to build familiarity and demonstrate support for education. All advertising still needs approval and must be appropriate for its audience.

Can I offer my own product as a prize?

Yes, subject to approval. The admin team must first review and approve your product and create its listing on Know To Win. Sponsorship can begin only after the product has been onboarded to the platform’s prize catalog.

Does sponsoring prizes include unlimited free advertising?

No. Prize inventory and advertising are separate. Ads require eligible unused stock and a sufficient purchased-credit balance. View and click charges depend on current prices and your sponsor discounts.

What happens when my sponsored prizes are used?

As units are allocated to prize orders, your remaining stock falls. You leave a product’s current sponsor listing when none of your units remain for that product. When all your sponsored stock is allocated, new ad selection stops until you replenish it.

How are messages for younger learners handled?

Every creative needs review and an approved age range. Creatives approved for younger audiences require an approved platform-hosted landing page. External links from that experience require additional platform approval and a leaving notice.

What can I measure?

Sponsor reporting includes views, clicks, daily and per-message breakdowns, and credit usage. These are recorded ad interactions; they do not represent guaranteed unique people, purchases, or sales.

Am I sponsoring an individual child?

You sponsor units in the platform’s prize inventory. This is a way to support learning rewards across the platform, rather than an arrangement to fund a named child.
