Zvaunogona kuita neBhokisi reMazano

Pfungwa inogona kutanga nedambudziko remazuva ose: mudziyo wakaoma kushandisa, basa risipo mumusha mako, chigadzirwa chinogona kuchinjirwa kuti chishande zviri nani, kana chiitiko chinobatsira icho nharaunda yako inoda kuronga. Haufaniri kutaura kuti wagadzira chinhu chisina kumbovapo.

Unogona kusarudza mukana we kugadzira chinhu chitsva, kugadzirisa chiripo kuti chienderane nezvinodiwa, basa renharaunda kana kupa zvinhu munharaunda . Chinangwa chako chinogona kuva che bhizinesi, nharaunda kana kudzidza. Pokutangira ipurojekiti yako, paunogona kutsanangura dambudziko nokushanda pariri.

Pano, kuita kuti pfungwa ive chinhu chaicho kunoreva kuitisa pfungwa yacho uye kunyora mugumisiro unobatsira. Izvi zvinogona kuva kugadzirisa chinhu, kupa chinhu, kuyedza muenzaniso wekutanga nemushandisi, kupedza chirongwa chenharaunda kana kudzidza kuti nzira yawanga waronga inofanira kuchinjwa. Bhokisi reMazano rinoronga chirongwa nemarekodhi acho; vanhu ndivo vachiri kuita basa chairo.

Unogona kutanga wakavanzika uye kuramba uchishanda uri wega. Kubudisa pachena, kutsvaga nhengo dzechikwata, kutsvaga chipangamazano, kuwana ongororo yenyanzvi uye kutsvaga mari inzira dzaungasarudza kana uchida. Chiyero chenharaunda kana danho reprojekiti hachisi chitupa chekuchengetedzeka, kuita purofiti kana kubudirira.

Wedzera zvidzidzo zvine chekuita naro, rubatsiro rwaunoda uye ruzivo rwekuchengetedzeka kwemidziyo kana rwakakodzera.

Zvidzoreso izvi zvine zvinangwa zvakasiyana; kuva nhengo yebasa hakuzvitsivi.

Tangira pano

Ongorora: nzwisisa dambudziko

Tsanangura zvinodiwa usati wasarudza mhinduro. Ndiani anosangana nedambudziko racho? Ari kuita sei parizvino? Nei nzira yako ingangobatsira? Ongorora mhinduro dziripo nemamwe mazano ane chekuita naro. Danho iri rinogona kuramba rakavanzika paunenge uchigadzira tsananguro yacho.

Ita kuti chiito chinotevera chive chakajeka

Ronga: kuronga mabasa anoshanda

Usati waenda padanho reRonga, gadzira basa rimwe chete risati ratanga kana kuti riri kuenderera mberi. Ipa basa munhu ane mutoro, mazuva parinobatsira, uye tsananguro yakajeka yezvinoreva kuripedza. Dimbura chinangwa chikuru kuita zviito zvaunogona kuedza zvechokwadi.

Dzidza usati wawedzera kuzvipira

Edza: ita chiedzo chidiki

Usati wapinda padanho reEdza, gadzirira chiedzo chakarongwa kana kuti chiri kuenderera mberi. Taura mubvunzo, chiito uye mugumisiro waunotarisira. Nyora zvakaitika chaizvo, kuti zvakanaka here, zvakaipa here kana kuti hazvina kupa mhinduro yakajeka, pamwe nezvawakadzidza. Mugumisiro usingafadzi unogona kuva chikonzero chokuchinja nzira.

Gadzirira chinhu chinogona kushandiswa

Gadzira: gadzira chigadzirwa kana nzira yokuchipa

Iva nebasa riri kuitwa uye tsanangura kwaunowana zviwanikwa muPfupiso yeBhizinesi: hapana zvimwe zviwanikwa zvinodiwa, zvako pachako, zvakagoveranwa, rubatsiro rwezvinhu kana mabasa panzvimbo yemari, kana mari inobva kunze. Purojekiti yemidziyo inodawo tsamba yokuchengetedzeka. Kunyora kuti uchashandisa mari yokunze sechinhu chinodiwa hakurevi kuti wawana mari yacho.

Jekesa chinopiwa nenzira yokuchipa

Parura: gadzirira kupa

Tsanangura zvauri kupa, vashandisi kana vatengi, nzira yokuchipa uye kwaunowana zviwanikwa. Pfupiso yeBhizinesi inokubatsirawo kufunga nezvedzimwe sarudzo, mari inodiwa, mutengo wokutengesa nawo, rubatsiro uye nguva yokuwana nokushandisa mari. Ongororo yeChipo inoronga mashoko ako kuti uaongorore nokuakopa; haiburitsi chitoro kana kugamuchira maodha.

Nyora zvakaitika zvechokwadi

Mhanyisa: nyora kuendeswa kwezvinhu kana maodha

Usati waenda padanho reMhanyisa, nyora chiitiko chokutanga chokupa, kutengesa kwokutanga kana kutengesa zvakare, kana kuti nguva yokuzivisa ine zvinhu zvakapihwa kana maodha. Ramba uchivandudza purojekiti nezvawakapa, mari yazvakadya nezvawakadzidza. Marekodhi aya anonyorwa nomuridzi; danho iri hariongorori roga kana wawana mari kana purofiti.

Rwendo haruna kumanikidzwa kufamba nenzira imwe chete

Kubva paMhanyisa unogona kudzokera paRonga kana paEdza. Paunodzokera pakuronga kana pakuedza pazvinenge zvakakodzera, unofanira kutsanangura chikonzero.

Nzvimbo dzokugadzirira kutanga kushanda dzakafanana kumapurojekiti ebhizinesi, enharaunda uye okudzidza. Unogonawo kuvhara purojekiti uchiti yapera padanho ripi neripi rekare kana izvozvo zvichitsanangura mugumisiro wayo nemazvo. Hapana chinoda kuti chirongwa choga choga chokudzidza chishandurwe bhizinesi.

Muenzaniso wakatsanangurwa: basa rokugadzirisa mabhasikoro munharaunda

Uyu muenzaniso wokuratidza kuti munhu angashandisa sei maturusi aya, hausi kutaura kuti chirongwa chakadai chakaitika kana kuti chakabudirira.

  1. Ongorora: Vadzidzi vanofamba kure kuti vagadziriswe mabhasikoro nyore. Tsanangura dambudziko, dzimwe nzira dziripo uye basa rokugadzirisa mabhasikoro munharaunda rauri kuronga.
  2. Ronga: Wedzera mabasa okuona kugadziriswa kunonyanya kudiwa, kunyora maturusi uye kutaura nevanogona kushandisa basa racho.
  3. Edza: Edza musangano mudiki wokugadzirisa. Nyora kudiwa kwacho, nguva yakatorwa, zvipingamupinyi nemhinduro dzevanhu. Chinja nzira kana chiedzo chisina kupa mhinduro yakajeka.
  4. Gadzira: Gadzirira nzvimbo nematurusi, govera mabasa uye tsanangura zviwanikwa. Wedzera zvinhu zvinofanira kucherechedzwa nezvekuchengetedzeka.
  5. Parura: Nyora zvakajeka zvauri kupa, vanozoshandisa basa racho, mutengo wauri kufunga nemari yaunoda, kuti vanhu vachariwana sei uye kuti rubatsiro ruchashanda sei.
  6. Mhanyisa: Nyora kuendeswa kwezvinhu kana kutengesa kwaitika zvechokwadi, wozoramba uchinyora nguva nenguva zvokugadzirisa, vatengi, mari nenguva. Wedzera humbowo pazvinenge zvichibatsira.

Chipangamazano, nhengo yechikwata kana ongororo yezvemari zvinogona kutsigira chirongwa ichi, asi hapana chimwe chazvo chinogara chichidiwa kuti uchinje matanho aya. Muridzi anogonawo kumira kana basa racho risingabatsiri kana mhinduro yakakodzera yatovapo.

Basa rakavanzika nekubudiswa pachena

Gwaro rako haringobudiswi pachena roga

Shanduro yauri kushanda nayo ndeyako uye ndeyevatori vechikamu vari munzvimbo yebasa vane mvumo parizvino. Kuti ugoverane pfungwa nenhengo dzenharaunda, zadzisa zvinodiwa pakuendesa, bvuma kubudiswa kwemashoko uye uendese kuti iongororwe isati yabudiswa. Mutariri anotendera kana kuramba shanduro yatumirwa.

Kana ukagadzirisa pfungwa yakatobudiswa, shanduro yokupedzisira yakatenderwa inoramba ichionekwa apo itsva yakavanzika kana kuti yakamirira kuongororwa. Kutenderwa kwepfungwa pachena hakungobudisi humbowo hwayo, zvapupu kana zvimwe zvakapiwa pachena zvoga.

Nyatsofunga zvaunobudisa

Zvakabudiswa zvinowanikwa kunhengo dzeBhokisi reMazano dzine mvumo. Marekodhi ebhizinesi, mashoko ekuongorora akavanzika, mishumo uye mashoko emupi akagoverwa pachibvumirano anoonekwa nevanhu vashoma. Usaise zvakavanzika, mashoko akavanzika okukufonera kana zvausina mvumo yokubudisa muchikumbiro cheruzhinji. Kuchengeta kana kubudisa pfungwa hakunyoresi patent kana kugadzira chibvumirano chokuti mashoko arambe akavanzika.

Mashoko okutanga neshanduro

Unogona kunyora mashoko nemutauro wako. Maonero akashandurirwa anobatsira vamwe kuaverenga, asi kushandura kunogona kutora nguva kana kusawanikwa. Mashoko epakutanga ndiwo anoramba ari rekodhi yakanyorwa. Kutsvaga kwevanhu munharaunda kunogona kushandisa zita repakutanga rakatenderwa paruzhinji uye zita raro reChirungu kana riripo; zita idzva rakavanzika kana risati ratenderwa harionekwi pakutsvaga kweruzhinji.

Kana chikumbiro chikarambwa kana kuti chikabviswa, chigadzirise uye chengeta shanduro itsva yebasa usati wachiendesa zvakare. Kubvisa shanduko yakamirira kuongororwa hakubvisi chinhu chakambobudiswa chichitenderwa.

Marekodhi anoshandura chinangwa kuita basa

Mabasa nezviedzo

Mabasa anotsanangura basa rinofanira kuitwa uye mutoro waro. Muridzi nevarairidzi vakapiwa basa vanogona kuronga mabasa; muridzi kana munhu akapiwa basa anogona kunyora mafambiro aro. Kupedza basa kunoda chinyorwa, uye kurega basa kunoda chikonzero. Zviedzo zvinotsanangura chinhu chokudzidza: mubvunzo, chiito chakarongwa, mugumisiro unotarisirwa uye wakaitika chaizvo. Varidzi nenhengo dzechikwata vanogona kugadzira zviedzo; muridzi kana munyori wacho ndiye anotarisira cheedza chiripo.

Pfupiso yeBhizinesi neOngororo yeChipo

Muridzi anonyora kuti ndiani anoshandisa kana kubhadhara mugumisiro, dzimwe nzira dziripo, zvaari kupa, mitengo nemari inodiwa, kuendesa, rubatsiro, nguva yemari nezviwanikwa. Kana fomu richibvumira, mari isingazivikanwi inogona kusiiwa isina kuzivikanwa. Pfupiso iyi chishandiso chokuronga, haisi hurongwa hwebhizinesi hunongogadzirwa neapp.

Zvinangwa zvinokosha nenguva dzemishumo

Nyora sechinangwa chinokosha zvipo zvapihwa, kuendesa kwokutanga, kutengesa kwokutanga uye kutengesa zvakare. Nguva dzemishumo dzinogona kurekodha maodha, zvinhu zvakaendeswa, vatengi, vatengi vanodzoka, kutengesa, mari yadzoserwa, mari yakashandiswa, mari yakatorwa nomuridzi uye maawa akashandiswa. Nguva dzazvino hadzigoni kupindirana. Gadzirisa chinyorwa netsananguro kana kuchibvisa nechikonzero. Mari yakasara inoratidzwa inoverengwa kubva pamashoko ako; haisi gwaro rezvemari rakaongororwa.

Mafaira akavanzika anodzivirirwa; kubudisa humbowo pachena kunochinja nemaune vanhu vanogona kuuona. Koka vamwe kana basa rava kuvada

Tsvaga vanhu vebasa rakajeka

  1. Muridzi anogadzira basa rine zita, chinyorwa nenhamba yezvigaro zviripo, ozovhura kunyorera.
  2. Nhengo inokodzera inonyorera basa iroro rakavhurika repfungwa yeruzhinji uye inonyora mashoko echikumbiro.
  3. Muridzi anoongorora zvikumbiro ozvigamuchira kana kuzviramba. Kugamuchira kunoongorora kuti basa richakavhurika here, nzvimbo ichiripo here uye zvinodiwa zvazvino zvichiri kuzadziswa here.
  4. Kugamuchira kunopinda munhu muve nhengo yechikwata ipapo ipapo. Munyoreri haafaniri kuita chimwe chiito chokupinda.

Vanonyorera vanotevera marekodhi avo muBhokisi reRubatsiro. Chikumbiro chakamirira chinogona kubviswa. Nhengo inogona kubva, uye muridzi anogona kubvisa nhengo, zvichida chikonzero chinodiwa. Munhu anonyorazve pashure pemugumisiro wokupedzisira anotevedzera mitemo iripo yebasa, nzvimbo uye kukodzera.

Koka chipangamazano

Chipangamazano anochengeta nhoroondo ine nzvimbo dzeunyanzvi nemitauro yaakakumbira. Maneja ndiye anosarudza nzvimbo dzinotenderwa nedzinobvumirwa kuongororwa nadzo. Muridzi anotsvaga chipangamazano akasarudzwa otumira kokero yepurojekiti iyoyo munzvimbo yakatenderwa. Chipangamazano anobvuma kana kuramba ari muBhokisi reRubatsiro.

Kokero yoga haivhuri nzvimbo yebasa yakavanzika. Basa rakagamuchirwa uye richine mvumo pari zvino ndiro rinopa mukana. Muridzi kana chipangamazano anogona kupedza basa riri kushanda, uye maneja anogona kuribvisa. Kumiswa kwenhoroondo kana kupera kwehukama hunodiwa kunogona kuvhara mukana kunyange peji rakambovhurwa.

Nhaurirano nemarekodhi okurairana

Vanhu vanogona kuvhura nhaurirano yepurojekiti kana paine vatori vechikamu vakakwana vanokodzera parizvino. Inoshandisa mameseji eKnow To Win, iine mitemo yayo yokuongorora, yokutarisirwa nomuchengeti, yokumiswa uye yokuvhara vamwe. Kubvisa munhu kana kupedza mvumo yake kunokanganisawo nhaurirano yepurojekiti.

Muridzi kana chipangamazano akapiwa basa anogona kunyora musangano wokurairana, vakapinda, manotsi, zviito zvinotevera uye zvinongedzo kumabasa. Kana uchida, unogona kubatanidza chirevo chemusangano wechidzidzo chinobhadharwa kuti upe mamiriro; izvi hazvishanduri purojekiti kuva bhuku rokubhukha chidzidzo kana kugadzira mubhadharo wechipangamazano.

Ongororo dzenyanzvi dzinoitwa nevanhu: nzwisisa maonero acho mumamiriro azvo

Nzvimbo dzeongororo ndedzemidziyo, mapurogiramu emakombiyuta, dhizaini, mari, mutemo, kugadzira zvinhu uye nharaunda. Muongorori anofanira kuva chipangamazano akapiwa basa parizvino uye aine mvumo yakakodzera yokuongorora; kunyora nhoroondo yako wega hakuna kukwana.

  1. Ona zviri kuongororwa. Zvinosanganisira pfungwa nemabasa azvino, uye ruzivo rwebhizinesi kana iri ongororo yezvemari.
  2. Muongorori anotumira maonero ake. Zvinosanganisira chinyorwa chezviri kuongororwa, mutongo wokuti—enderera mberi, gadzirisa kana mira—uye zvinogona kusanganisira njodzi, zviito zvinokurudzirwa uye kuzivisa kana paine kusawirirana kwezvido.
  3. Muridzi anoverenga uye angasarudza kuatora semaonero ari kushanda. Kuatambira kuti ave maonero aripo kunoda kuti shanduro yepurojekiti yakaongororwa ichiri yazvino uye kuti muongorori achine mvumo. Kana zviri kuongororwa zvachinja, zvingada ongororo itsva.

Kuatambira kunoreva kurekodha ongororo iyoyo semaonero ari kushanda iye zvino munzvimbo yayo. Hazvirevi kuti mutongo wose wakagamuchirwa unoti “enderera mberi”, uye zviito zvinokurudzirwa hazvingochinji zvoga kuva mabasa. Muridzi haagoni kuongorora pfungwa yake. Mashoko eongororo anovanzika kuvanhu vane mvumo; mashoko ezvemari anogona kugoverwa nenzira yakasiyana yokutumira chikumbiro chemari pachibvumirano.

Ongororo inobatsira pakuita sarudzo; haisi shanduko yechikamu inongoitika, vimbiso yeunyanzvi kana kuti chinotsiva zano rinodiwa rokuchengetedzeka nemutemo. Ongororo dzepamutemo hadzidiwi nguva dzose kuti munhu agadzire kana kutanga kupa. Ongororo yezvemari yazvino yakagamuchirwa ndiyo inodiwa pachikumbiro chemari.

Dzidza kubva munharaunda

  • Mashoko: Wedzera zvawaona nemibvunzo, kusanganisira mhinduro kumashoko akabudiswa.
  • Zviyero: Ipa chiyero cheutsva, kugona kuitika, kuchengetedzeka, kukosha pakudzidza uye kubatsira munharaunda kubva pa1 kusvika pa5, kana kuti sarudza “hapana chiyero” pane chausingagoni kutonga. Chiyero chepamusoro chokuchengetedzeka chinoreva kuti chakachengeteka zvikuru. Chimwe chezvinhu chinofanira kuva nechiyero chenhamba.
  • Pfungwa dzine chekuita nedzimwe nezvichemo zvokufanana: Tsanangura hukama neimwe pfungwa yakabudiswa pachena. Chichemo chokuti zvakafanana chinoongororwa; hachibatanidzi mapurojekiti oga.
  • Mhinduro dzatovapo pamusika: Tsanangura mhinduro iripo uye kuti inopedza zvinodiwa zvacho zvose here kana kuti zvimwe chete. Mhinduro iripo inogona kuramba ichisiya nzvimbo yebasa romunharaunda kana kugadziriswa kunobatsira.
  • Zvapupu: Tsanangura ukama hwako nemugumisiro wacho nezvawakaitirwa nawo. Zvapupu zvenhengo yechikwata kana chipangamazano zvinoda kuti ukama hwacho huenderane; muridzi haagoni kupupura nezvepfungwa yake.
  • Humbowo: Tumira manotsi, zvinongedzo kana mafaira anotsigira anokodzera kuti aongororwe.
  • Mishumo: Taura nyaya ine chekuita nokuvanzika, zvinhu zvisina kuchengetedzeka, kushungurudza, tsamba dzisina kukumbirwa, kudzokorora kana imwe nyaya inotsigirwa. Tevera mushumo wako muMishumo Yangu.

Munhu mumwe chete ndiye anovhota papfungwa imwe neimwe; kuchinja vhoti yako kunovandudza yacho. Migumisiro inoratidza nhamba iri pakati yezviyero zvechinhu chimwe nechimwe nenhamba yeavo vakapa chiyero nenhamba yevasina kupa, kwete chiyero chimwe chokubudirira. Nhamba iri pakati inogona kubva pazviyero zvishoma, saka verenga nhamba dzacho pamwe chete nayo.

Kuongororwa kwemipiro kwakaparadzana nokubudiswa kwepfungwa. Mutariri anogona kubudisa, kuramba kana kuviga mipiro inokodzera. Mishumo inoongororwa; kutumira mushumo hakuvigi chinhu chataurwa ipapo ipapo. Kubatanidza zvakafanana kunorambidzwa kune zvimwe zviitiko uye kunogadzira chinongedzo chepurojekiti yakasarudzwa sepakutanga, hakusi kuendesa basa rechikwata chakagadzirwa mune rimwe projekiti chinyararire.

Kuendeswa kumupi wemari kana uchida, nokugoverana mashoko nemaune

Bhokisi reMazano rinogona kuronga kuendeswa kupurojekiti kumupi wemari akatenderwa uye anokodzera. Parizvino rudzi rwemupi iNBFC—kambani yezvemari isiri bhanga muIndia—uye mari inokumbirwa inoverengwa nemarupiya ekuIndia. Iyi inzira yakaparadzana nezvikumbiro zvezvikwereti zvedzidzo.

  1. Wana uye gamuchira ongororo yezvemari yazvino. Kugadzira chikumbiro kunotoda ongororo iyoyo, mari yaunokumbira, mashandisirwo ayo uye kushomeka kwezviwanikwa.
  2. Vhura chikumbiro kana uchikodzera. Kuchivhura kunoda muridzi mukuru uye purojekiti neongororo zvichiri kushanda uye zvichigona kushandiswa.
  3. Sarudza mupi wemari uye tarisa mashoko acho usati waagovera. Mashoko okutanga ane pfupiso shoma; mashoko akazara ane rumwe ruzivo rwepurojekiti, mabasa, bhizinesi neongororo.
  4. Bvumira kugoverwa kwemashoko chaiwo anoratidzwa. Kugoverana kunongoshanda kumupi akasarudzwa neshanduro yemashoko awatarisa. Kuvhura chikumbiro hakubudisi nzvimbo yebasa yakavanzika kumunhu wose.
  5. Puratifomu inoratidza uye inopa chikumbiro kumupi. Kuratidza chikumbiro kunoda mvumo yemashoko okutanga. Kuchipa kumupi kunoda mvumo yemashoko akazara. Mupi anogona kuzobvuma chikumbiro chaanzi agamuchire, kana kuramba kana kusvetuka chikumbiro chichiri kushanda uye chaanokodzera.
  6. Tevera zvakazoitika kana kumisa kugoverana mashoko. Muridzi anogona kubvisa mvumo kana kuvhara/kubvisa chikumbiro, achipa chikonzero chinodiwa. Kuendeswa kuchiri kushanda kwakabatana nacho kunobviswa. Marekodhi evapi asisiri kushanda anochengeta mamiriro azvo asi haapi mukana unoramba uripo kuzviri mukati mepurojekiti zvakagoverwa.

Kuchinja mashoko echikumbiro kunoita kuti mvumo dzokugovera dzakapfuura dzisashande. Ongororo yakwegura, kusakodzera kwemupi, kumiswa kwepurojekiti kana kubviswa kwemvumo zvinogona kuita kuti mashoko asawanikwe. Mvumo inotariswa zvakare nguva imwe neimwe; kuwana kwakamboitika hakupi mvumo yechigarire.

Kubvuma kuendeswa kunorekodha mhinduro yomupi. Hakusi kutamisa mari, kubvumidza chikwereti, kutara mashoko okubhadhara kana kuvimbisa mari. Hurukuro pamusoro pemari nechibvumirano chipi nechipi chezvemari zvinoitika kunze kwenzira iyi. Mamiriro okuti ongororo yezvemari yakagamuchirwa haarevi hazvo kuti mutongo wayo waive wakanaka.

Vachengeti nekuchengetedzeka

Muchengeti mukuru akabatana parizvino uye anokodzera anogona kuvhura Bhokisi reMazano ari pakuona kweMuchengeti weVana (Guardian Kids) kwemwana. Muchengeti haafaniri kutenga nhengo yake yeBhokisi reMazano kuti atarisire mwana uyu.

Peji iri rinoratidza ruzivo rwepurojekiti nemabasa ane chekuita nemwana anotenderwa, zvakadai semabasa akapiwa mwana, zviedzo kana humbowo hwaakanyora, uye marekodhi emisangano yokurairana yaakapinda. Haripi mukana usina muganhu kumarekodhi ebhizinesi echikwata chose, mafaira akavanzika kana mameseji, uye haritenderi muchengeti kugadzirisa semwana.

Muchengeti anogona kukumbira kuti kubviswa kwechinhu kuongororwe, achipa chikonzero. Chikumbiro chacho chinova mushumo unoongororwa nomutariri ane mvumo; hachibvisi kana kumisa pfungwa yacho. Mameseji anogonawo kudzimwa nemitemo yokutarisira yakazvimirira pakuva nhengo yeBhokisi reMazano.

Kuvandudza nhengo, mamiriro achinja uye kuvhara

Kana nguva yenhengo yapera

Nhoroondo yepurojekiti yako yakachengetwa zvakajeka inongoverengwa chete, uye nhoroondo dzako dzinotsigirwa nezviito zvokubuda zvinoramba zviripo maererano nemitemo yazvo. Kupera kwenguva hakuchengeti mukana wose wokutsvaga munharaunda kana wenzvimbo yebasa yakavanzika yomumwe munhu. Shandisa sarudzo yenhengo kuti utarise muripo wazvino uye uvandudze nemaune uchishandisa makiredhiti akatengwa. Kuvandudza kunodzorera zviito zvebasa zvinokodzera, asi hakugadziri zvakare basa rechikwata rakabviswa kana basa rechipangamazano rakapera.

Kana peji rako risisina mashoko azvino

Chimwe chiito chinogona kuchinja purojekiti fomu rakavhurika. Kana app ikakukumbira kuti utangezve peji pashure pokunge shanduko dzapokana, ongorora mamiriro matsva epurojekiti usati waedza zvakare. Mvumo inogona kuchinjawo nokubviswa kwebasa, kubviswa kwemvumo, kupera kwenhengo kana kumiswa. Kuvhura peji kare hakupi mvumo yokuramba uchishandisa mashoko akavanzika.

Kana purojekiti yapera

Muridzi anogona kuvhara purojekiti achiti yapera, yamiswa, mhinduro iripo kana kuti haigoni kuitika, achinyora chikonzero. Kuvhara kunomisa kutsvaga nhengo kwakavhurika uye kunopedza zviitiko zvemari zvichiri kushanda zvine chekuita nayo. Chinhu chakambobudiswa chichitenderwa chinogona kuramba chichionekwa. Muridzi haana murayiro wokuvhurazve purojekiti yakavharwa, saka nyatsoongorora chisarudzo usati wachisimbisa.

Kudzidza kuti nzira haina kukodzera kuchiri kudzidza kunobatsira. Nyora zvakaitika nechikonzero chawachinjira nzira pane kutaura kubudirira kusingatsigirwi nebasa racho.

Mibvunzo inowanzo bvunzwa

Ndinofanira kuva nechikwata, chipangamazano kana mutsigiri wezvemari ndisati ndatanga here?

Kwete. Tanga uri muridzi wepurojekiti yakavanzika yaunoita uri wega. Zvikwata, kurairana, ongororo dzevanhu uye kuendeswa kumupi wemari inzira dzokutsigirwa dzaungasarudza dzine zvadzinoda.

Ndinofanira kubudisa pfungwa yangu pachena kuti ndironge kana kushanda pairi here?

Kwete. Mabasa, zviedzo, Pfupiso yeBhizinesi uye marekodhi emafambiro akavanzika zvinotsigira basa risati rabudiswa. Kutsvaga nhengo pachena nezviito zvenharaunda zvine zvimwe zvinodiwa zvokubudisa uye kukodzera.

Chiyero chakakwirira kana ongororo yakagamuchirwa inoita kuti purojekiti yangu ibudirire here?

Kwete. Zviyero maonero enharaunda. Ongororo maonero evanhu ane zvinangwa zvakatemwa. Kuendesa zvinhu nemarekodhi ezvemari zvinonyorwa nomuridzi. Hapana chimwe chezvinhu izvi chinosimbisa chakazvimirira kubudirira, kuchengetedzeka, purofiti kana kuwana mari.

Kushandisa Bhokisi reMazano kunodzivirira zviri pamutemo chinhu chandakagadzira here?

Kuchengeta gwaro nekubudisa pachena zvichiongororwa hakunyoresi patent kana kugadzira chibvumirano chokuchengetedza mashoko. Sarudza zvaunogona kubudisa uye tsvaga zano rakakodzera kana kodzero dzepfungwa dzichikosha.

Chipangamazano anogona kugara achiona basa rangu rakavanzika here?

Kwete. Kokero haisi mvumo yokupinda. Kodzero dzokushandisa nzvimbo yebasa dzinoenderana nebasa rakagamuchirwa richiri kushanda, nzvimbo yakatenderwa uye kumwe kuongorora kukodzera. Kupedza kana kubvisa hukama kunobvisa mvumo iyoyo kunze kwokunge humwe hukama huripo huchiri kupa mukana hwahwo.

Rondedzero yezviito zvinotevera ndiyo nzira yandinofanira kutevedzera here?

Kwete. Inotsanangura marudzi ezviito zvinowanikwa. Muridzi ndiye anosarudza basa, uye shanduko yedanho inoongorora zvinodiwa zvayo kana yakumbirwa.
